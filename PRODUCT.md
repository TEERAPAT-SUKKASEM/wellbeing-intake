# Product context

Campus Health & Wellbeing is a student support intake and appointment prototype. Students explore four campus services, send a private request, and book after practitioner review. Emergency contacts must remain accessible and independent of backend availability. The coordinator sees metadata only; practitioner reads are audited.

This workspace is a frontend sample for visual review. The backend belongs to the user's collaborator. UI_PREVIEW=true enables fictional services, a request form, and a sample booking/cancellation dashboard. Nothing is sent or persisted in preview. Do not imply real appointments or real campus facilities are pictured.
