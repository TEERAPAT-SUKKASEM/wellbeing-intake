# Product context

Campus Health & Wellbeing is an anonymous counselling appointment prototype. Students choose a service and time, submit a private request without signing in, and track or cancel it with a Private Access Key. Practitioners approve, reschedule, reject, or cancel; administrators manage services and weekly opening windows. Every session has a mandatory 15-minute privacy buffer. Emergency contacts remain static and independent of backend availability.

The backend belongs to the user's collaborator. Frontend work preserves its API contract, database schema, and staff authentication. UI_PREVIEW=true enables fictional services, intake, key confirmation, tracking, and cancellation in development. Student preview data stays in memory and resets on refresh; staff screens continue to require the real API. Do not imply real appointments or real campus facilities are pictured. The current behavior is specified by wellbeing_prd.md, docs/PRD.md, docs/BACKEND-HANDOFF.md, and openapi.yaml.
